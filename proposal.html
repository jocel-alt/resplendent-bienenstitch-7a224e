The Challenge
When AI agents
aren't built right

Most small businesses launch their AI with zero formal testing. They discover failures the same way their customers do — live, in public, after the damage is done.

01
Invents Facts Under Pressure

Prices, hours, policies, product specs — when it doesn't know, it guesses confidently. Your reputation pays the price.

02
Can Be Talked Out of Its Rules

A polite but persistent customer can convince it to discount, disclose, or override the policies it was told to enforce.

03
Leaks Sensitive Information

System prompts, internal notes, other customers' data — these slip out when probed, even casually. One screenshot can go viral.

04
Mishandles Upset Customers

It escalates the wrong way, sounds robotic in moments needing empathy, or argues with the customer when they need care.

05
No Safe Fallback

When it hits an unknown, it doesn't hand off to a human — it improvises. That's where bookings are lost and trust breaks.

06
Treats Every Input as Trusted

Prompt injection, jailbreaks, and role-play attacks aren't blocked because the build never accounted for them.

Common Questions
Frequently asked
questions
01How is this different from asking my AI vendor to test it?

Your vendor has a vested interest in saying their product works. I'm a neutral third party — I work for you, not the platform. Every finding is reported without bias, with evidence.

02Do I need to take my AI offline during testing?

No. I test against your live AI exactly as a customer would — without disrupting your real operations. Your business keeps running throughout.

03What if you find major problems?

Critical issues are flagged to you within 24 hours of discovery — you don't wait for the final report. I'll also recommend specific, achievable fixes in plain language.

04Will my data and conversations be kept confidential?

Absolutely — and it's written into the agreement. NDA signed before any credentials are shared. Test recordings retained 90 days, then permanently deleted. Your business details are never used in marketing without your written permission.

05Can I upgrade tiers mid-engagement?

Yes. If we start with Basic and find issues warranting deeper testing, you can upgrade to Standard or Premium and only pay the difference.
