What I Test
Six failure modes
I catch before your customers do

Every AI agent I test is evaluated across six dimensions — the same ones that cause real-world brand damage when left unchecked.

🎯
Factual Accuracy

Does it state the right prices, hours, policies, and product details? Or does it confidently guess when it doesn't know?

🛡️
Security & Prompt Injection

Can it be tricked into revealing your system prompt, internal data, or other customers' information through adversarial inputs?

🎙️
Brand Voice & Tone

Does it sound like your brand? Is it warm where it should be warm, firm where it should be firm? Does it stay on-character under pressure?

💬
Edge Cases & Unusual Inputs

What happens when a customer types in another language, asks something completely off-topic, or sends a confusing multi-part question?

📞
Escalation & Fallback

When the AI doesn't know, does it hand off gracefully — or does it keep improvising? Does it know when a human needs to step in?

💡
Policy Compliance

Can it be talked into discounting, overriding rules, or making promises you didn't authorise? I test boundary-pushing conversations directly.

Common Questions
Frequently asked
questions
01How is this different from asking my AI vendor to test it?▾

Your vendor has a vested interest in saying their product works. I'm a neutral third party — I work for you, not the platform. Every finding is reported without bias, backed by verbatim evidence.

02Do I need to take my AI offline during testing?▾

No. I test against your live AI exactly as a customer would — without disrupting your real operations. Your business keeps running throughout.

03What if you find major problems?▾

Critical issues are flagged to you within 24 hours of discovery — you don't wait for the final report. I'll also recommend specific, achievable fixes in plain language your developer can act on immediately.

04Will my data and conversations be kept confidential?▾

Absolutely — and it's written into the agreement. NDA signed before any credentials are shared. Test recordings are retained for 90 days, then permanently deleted. Your business details are never used in marketing without your written permission.

05What types of AI agents do you test?▾

Chat-based agents (website widgets, WhatsApp, Instagram DMs, etc.) and voice AI agents. If your AI handles customer conversations in any channel, I can test it.

06Can I upgrade tiers mid-engagement?▾

Yes. If we start with Basic and find issues warranting deeper testing, you can upgrade to Standard or Premium and only pay the difference.
